# Chosen Direction — Tidal Signal

## Design Movement

**Cinematic editorial civic-tech**: a union of documentary field photography, contemporary Indian editorial design, and low-friction public infrastructure. The supplied violet desert film is a visual atmosphere rather than decoration; it sets a quiet, expansive mood for decisions that matter.

## Core Principles

1. **People before platforms:** Describe outcomes for real communities, not features or technological novelty.
2. **Clarity through rhythm:** Let a generous editorial hierarchy, numbered pathways, and concise utility language simplify complexity.
3. **Atmosphere with restraint:** Use dark, cinematic environments to create focus; texture, grain and muted color carry depth instead of gradients or effects.
4. **Action must feel safe:** Each pathway should be an unmistakable invitation into a focused assistance conversation, with visible scope and practical prompt starters.

## Color Philosophy

The background world is **aubergine night**, taken from the user’s desert video, so the platform feels attentive rather than corporate. **Bone paper** brings legibility and human warmth to content panels. **Muted chartreuse** is the ownable signal color: rare, directional, and used only where a choice or action needs to register. Clay, smoke, and violet tints retain a material connection to land, weather, and craft.

## Layout Paradigm

The homepage is a **field broadcast** rather than a centered marketing funnel. A fixed editorial masthead sits above a full-screen video entry. The nine pathways unfold as a numbered **signal index**—a meandering, asymmetrical list with alternating vertical weight rather than a generic feature grid. A selected pathway opens a conversation dock over the index, retaining the user’s place and letting the transition feel intentional.

## Signature Elements

1. A hovering **signal dial** in the hero: its rotating perimeter maps attention from 01 to 09.
2. **Offset index bars**: each pathway combines a large number, a narrow category line, and a sentence of real-world value.
3. A small **field-note tag** used for language, urgency, and connection context—not as decorative badges.

## Interaction Philosophy

The page should feel like opening a well-designed public service, not browsing a pitch deck. Hovering a pathway reveals its practical brief; activating it opens a focused chat panel with suggested questions. Keyboard focus remains obvious, interactions are immediate, and every overlay has an easy exit. Motion is used to explain transitions and preserve orientation—not to entertain.

## Animation

On entry, the hero title, mission statement, and signal dial resolve upward in 40–70ms staggers, using opacity and transform only. The video plays muted and loops behind a static contrast veil. On path hover, the number rises by 3px and a fine signal line extends; on click, the content dock slides from the lower right with a 220ms custom ease-out. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: no entrance animations, no rotating dial, and no background video playback controls beyond a static poster fallback.

## Typography System

**DM Serif Display** delivers a literary, human headline voice and is used only for section titles and key narrative phrases. **Manrope** handles interface, pathway names, metadata, and chat copy for resilient readability. Headlines are compact but large, with tight tracking; all functional labels use wide tracking, upper-case text and deliberate line-height. No Inter.

## Brand Essence

**Collective Signal is a community-first gateway to practical AI guidance for people often left out of technology’s promise.**

Personality: **grounded, dignified, watchful**.

## Brand Voice

Headlines speak in plain, image-led phrases. Calls to action describe the specific next move, and microcopy acknowledges real conditions such as limited bandwidth or local language needs. Avoid abstraction, hype, and promises of perfection.

> “A clearer next step, wherever you work.”

> “Ask in the language you use every day.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark uses a compressed, letterspaced **COLLECTIVE SIGNAL** lockup paired with a hand-cut emblem: a three-part radial sprout-signal. The symbol suggests a shared source of information growing outward, rather than a robot or a chat bubble.

## Signature Brand Color

**Signal Chartreuse — #C4F25A**. It is reserved for decisive navigation, active selections, and essential emphasis against aubergine night.
